# Budgetly Privacy Policy

Welcome to Budgetly.

Budgetly is a personal finance management application operated by Sophie Kendi, an independent developer based in Kenya.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Account information

When you create or maintain an account, we may process your:

- Username and email address.
- First and last name, where provided.
- Password in securely hashed form.
- Account settings and preferences.
- Registration eligibility declaration.
- Records of acceptance of our Terms of Service and acknowledgement of our Privacy Policy.

### 2.2 Financial information

Budgetly stores information you choose to record, including:

- Financial account names, currencies and starting balances.
- Income, expenses and transaction details.
- Categories and budgets.
- Transfers between accounts.
- Savings goals, contributions, withdrawals and plans.
- Borrowing, lending, loan interest and repayment records.
- Financial notes and descriptions.

Some entries may contain information about other people, such as names recorded for personal loans. Please avoid adding information about others unless reasonably necessary and lawfully obtained.

### 2.3 Support and communication

When you request support or submit feedback, we may process your contact information, message, feedback category and relevant correspondence.

### 2.4 Payment information

Where paid Budgetly features are offered, payment-related information may be processed to complete purchases, administer access, address payment problems and meet applicable accounting or legal obligations.

The types of information processed, the payment provider and any billing records retained by Budgetly depend on the payment features actually enabled.

Budgetly will identify the applicable payment provider and payment-data practices before collecting payments.

### 2.5 Browser and technical information

Budgetly uses browser storage to support account sessions and save preferences, including appearance and onboarding settings.

Technical information may also be processed by our infrastructure for security, troubleshooting and service operation.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

- Register, authenticate and manage accounts.
- Provide requested financial tracking and reporting features.
- Calculate balances, budgets, savings progress, currency conversions and loan figures.
- Deliver account-related emails, including password recovery and welcome messages.
- Operate paid features where available.
- Respond to feedback and support requests.
- Prevent unauthorized access and maintain service security.
- Meet applicable legal obligations.
- Handle requests concerning personal information.

Depending on the activity and applicable law, processing may be necessary to perform our agreement with you, comply with legal obligations, pursue legitimate interests that do not override your rights, or rely on consent where required.

Acknowledging this Privacy Policy does not constitute blanket consent to unrelated processing.

## 4. Financial Information and Accuracy

Budgetly primarily processes financial records entered by users.

Budgetly does not independently verify that your recorded transactions, balances, loans or repayments match those held by financial institutions or other individuals.

The accuracy of financial reports depends on the information entered and the application's calculation rules.

## 5. Service Providers

Budgetly uses third-party infrastructure to deliver parts of its service.

These include:

**Alwaysdata:** Backend application hosting and related infrastructure.

**Brevo:** Configured transactional email delivery for messages such as welcome emails and password resets.

**Frankfurter:** Exchange-rate data used for supported currency conversions. Backend exchange-rate requests involve currency information rather than an intentional transfer of your complete financial records.

**Payment services:** If paid checkout is enabled, Budgetly will identify the payment provider and explain its relevant data-processing practices before you make a purchase.

Service providers may process information needed to perform their services. We do not authorize them to use personal information for unrelated purposes merely because they support Budgetly.

## 6. International Processing

Budgetly is operated from Kenya and is intended to be accessible internationally.

Some service providers may process or store information outside Kenya or the country where you live.

Where international transfers occur, we will apply the safeguards and legal requirements relevant to those transfers.

Different privacy laws may apply depending on your location and the nature of the processing.

## 7. Cookies and Browser Storage

Budgetly uses session storage for authentication tokens and local storage for certain interface preferences.

Budgetly's frontend does not currently include third-party advertising pixels or analytics SDKs.

Other technical cookies or identifiers may be used by supporting infrastructure, so we do not describe the entire service as cookie-free.

If we introduce optional analytics, advertising or other non-essential tracking, we will update our disclosures and provide any consent mechanisms required by law.

## 8. Email Communications

Budgetly sends operational emails associated with your account, such as welcome messages and password-reset instructions.

We do not currently use your email address for promotional marketing campaigns.

If we introduce marketing communications, we will provide appropriate information and choices, including consent where required.

## 9. Data Security

Budgetly uses measures intended to protect personal information, including password hashing, authenticated access to protected application features and controls limiting access to user-owned records.

We also work to maintain the reliability and integrity of the application.

No online system is completely risk-free. Users should protect their passwords and notify us of suspected unauthorized access.

## 10. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it is processed, subject to applicable legal obligations.

The following retention principles apply:

- **Account and financial records:** Kept while needed to maintain an active account and provide requested services.
- **Deleted accounts:** Successfully deleted accounts and associated user-owned financial records are removed from Budgetly's primary application database.
- **Payment records:** Where payments are enabled, relevant records may be kept for lawful accounting, tax, dispute or fraud-prevention purposes.
- **Feedback and support records:** Kept as needed to resolve requests and administer related issues.
- **Operational logs and backups:** Retained only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy and to meet applicable legal obligations.

Deletion from the primary database may not immediately erase information held in backups, technical logs, delivered emails or independent copies retained by service providers.

## 11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to:

- Be informed about how your information is used.
- Request access to information held about you.
- Request correction of inaccurate information.
- Request deletion where applicable.
- Object to certain processing.
- Request restriction of processing.
- Request portability of applicable information.
- Withdraw consent where processing depends on consent.

To make a request, email **budgetly01@gmail.com**.

We may request reasonable information to verify your identity before disclosing or changing personal records.

Requests will be reviewed and answered within applicable legal requirements.

Where relevant, you may also contact Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner at https://www.odpc.go.ke/.

## 12. Data Export and Account Deletion

Budgetly provides an account export feature covering the categories of information supported by the current export tool. It may not include every type of personal information held by the service.

You may request additional information using the privacy contact above.

You can initiate account deletion through Budgetly's Settings page. The process requires password confirmation.

Once successfully completed, deletion removes the account and its associated user-owned financial records from the primary application database. This action is intended to be irreversible.

You should export any records you wish to retain before deleting your account.

Residual copies and information subject to lawful retention are handled according to our retention practices.

## 13. Age Requirements

Budgetly is intended for adults aged 18 or older.

During registration, users must declare that they meet the minimum age requirement.

Budgetly does not currently independently verify age or identity.

If you believe someone under 18 has registered, contact budgetly01@gmail.com.
